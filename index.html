03 / UNDERSTAND

Why BST deletion feels tricky

The same operation follows three different rules depending on the node.

CASE 01

Zero children

Leaf node: remove it. No other links need to change.

● → ∅
CASE 02

One child

One subtree: connect the parent directly to the existing child.

● → ◉
CASE 03 · MOST SUBTLE

Two children

Two subtrees: replace the value with its in-order successor, then remove the successor's original node.

● → ✦
!
Why the in-order successor?

It's the smallest value in the deleted node's right subtree, so it fits between all values on the left and the remaining values on the right. This demo consistently uses the successor method; the predecessor method also works.
